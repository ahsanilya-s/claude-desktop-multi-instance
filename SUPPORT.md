# Getting help

- **How do I…?** Check the [README](README.md) and the
  [FAQ and troubleshooting page](https://ahsanilya-s.github.io/claude-desktop-multi-instance/faq.html), then ask in
  [Discussions](https://github.com/ahsanilya-s/claude-desktop-multi-instance/discussions).
- **Something is broken:** open a
  [bug report](https://github.com/ahsanilya-s/claude-desktop-multi-instance/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml), and
  include the `-List` output.
- **Security problem:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) to report it privately.
- **Claude Desktop itself**, such as sign-in, billing or app bugs that also happen without this tool:
  [Anthropic support](https://support.claude.com).

This is a volunteer project, so answers are best effort.
